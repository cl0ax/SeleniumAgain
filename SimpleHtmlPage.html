Classic Batman Characters

Batman / Bruce Wayne
Played by Adam West
The Caped Crusader himself, known for his deadpan delivery and iconic utility belt. Adam West's portrayal became legendary for its campy, straight-faced approach to the character.
Robin / Dick Grayson
Played by Burt Ward
Batman's trusty sidekick, famous for his enthusiastic exclamations like "Holy smoke, Batman!" and his acrobatic skills as the Boy Wonder.
The Joker
Played by Cesar Romero
Batman's arch-nemesis, known for his maniacal laugh and colorful schemes. Cesar Romero famously refused to shave his mustache, which was painted over with white makeup.
Catwoman
Played by Julie Newmar, Lee Meriwether, and Eartha Kitt
The feline fatale who walked the line between villain and anti-hero. Three different actresses portrayed this iconic character throughout the series.

The Batman TV series aired from 1966-1968 and became a cultural phenomenon with its distinctive "POW!" and "BAM!" action sequences.
